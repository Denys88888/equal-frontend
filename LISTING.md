## Long Description

Equal is the first dating app built natively for Pi Network's 69M+ Pioneers worldwide.

**Why Equal is different**
Traditional dating apps sell visibility — pay more, get more swipes. Equal flips that model entirely. Your compatibility score is calculated from shared interests, relationship goals, and values. No wallet advantages. No premium boosts. Your personality is your currency.

**Core features**

🔍 **Smart Matching** — Discover profiles ranked by real compatibility, not ad spend. Filter by interests, age, city, and relationship goals.

💡 **Compatibility Tab** — See your interest overlap score before you like someone. Make informed connections, not blind swipes.

🤝 **Clubs** — Join interest-based communities: Pi Travelers, Pi Foodies, Pi Tech, and more. Connect with people who share your world.

🗓 **Events** — Attend local and virtual meetups organized for the Pi community: Speed Dating Nights, Pi Pioneers Meetups, outdoor gatherings, and more.

💬 **Real-time Chat** — Message your matches instantly after a mutual like. No token required to start a conversation.

✅ **Verified Profiles** — Every user authenticates via Pi Network. No fake accounts, no bots, no catfishing.

🌍 **34 Languages** — Fully localized for Pi's global community, from English and Russian to Arabic, Chinese, and beyond.

🔒 **Privacy First** — Login with your Pi account. Equal does not store passwords, does not track you, and never sells your data.

**Free to use**
Browse, match, and message — completely free. Equal uses Pi Network's verified identity instead of paywalls.

Equal — your affection has no price.
